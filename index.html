FAQ

Do you guarantee a specific number of meetings?

No. We do not make performance claims we cannot substantiate. The pilot covers strategy, research, outreach execution and optimization. Results depend on offer, market, list quality, deliverability and sales follow-up.

Is this mass cold email?

No. The approach is intentionally narrower: qualify accounts, verify relevant context, prioritize the strongest signals, and then personalize outreach to the appropriate decision-maker.

Who is this built for?

Initially, founder-led B2B web design and development agencies with strong delivery, meaningful project values, and capacity for additional clients.

What happens after the 30-day pilot?

We review the data together. If the process is showing promise, we can discuss an ongoing engagement. There is no automatic long-term commitment.

Want us to map the first signals in your market?

Tell us what your agency sells and who your best clients are. We’ll use that to evaluate whether the pilot is a fit.
