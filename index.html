Why we exist

Make outbound more relevant, respectful and useful.

Great agencies should not have to choose between waiting on referrals and becoming a spam machine. Our mission is to help capable web agencies build a more intentional path to new business—one grounded in fit, timing and honest research.

We believe the best outbound feels less like interruption and more like good timing.

The goal is not to contact everyone. It is to earn attention from the right few by being relevant enough to start a useful conversation.

Relevance over volume

We would rather research 100 plausible accounts than spray 10,000 generic contacts.

Evidence over assumptions

Signals are hypotheses. We verify the facts before using them in a message.

Human judgment over blind automation

AI can accelerate research and drafting, but judgment stays in the loop where it matters.

Conversations over vanity metrics

Open rates and list size are secondary. The real objective is relevant replies and qualified sales conversations.

The SignalSourced method

Start with why now, then earn the conversation.

Our method is intentionally simple: understand the economics, define the right accounts, identify credible triggers, find the decision-maker, and write outreach around verified context.

Step 1 · Economics
Understand what a good client is worth.We clarify project value, capacity and where outbound can make commercial sense.
Step 2 · ICP
Define the companies worth pursuing.Industry, size, geography, use case, decision-maker and clear exclusion criteria.
Step 3 · Signals
Look for a reason the timing may matter.Funding, leadership changes, hiring, launches, expansion, rebrands or visible website/positioning gaps.
Step 4 · Research
Verify the account and the person.We validate the signal, identify the likely owner of the problem, verify contact data and score the opportunity.
Step 5 · Outreach
Start a relevant conversation.Short, contextual messaging, responsible follow-up and clear routing of interested replies.
Step 6 · Learn
Improve from real market feedback.We review reply quality, objections and conversion points, then refine the ICP, signals and message.

Questions

FAQ

Do you guarantee a specific number of meetings?

No. We do not make performance claims we cannot substantiate. The pilot covers strategy, research, outreach execution and optimization. Results depend on the offer, market, targeting, deliverability and sales follow-up.

Is this mass cold email?

No. The approach is intentionally narrower: qualify accounts, verify relevant context, prioritize stronger opportunities, then personalize outreach to the appropriate decision-maker.

Does a “buying signal” mean the company definitely wants a new website?

No. A signal is a research trigger, not proof of intent. We use it to form a better hypothesis about timing and then verify the surrounding context before outreach.

Who is this built for?

Initially, founder-led B2B web design and development agencies with strong delivery, meaningful project values and capacity for additional clients.

What happens after the 30-day pilot?

We review the data together. If the process is showing promise, we can discuss an ongoing engagement. There is no automatic long-term commitment.
